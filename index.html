FAQ

Everything You Need
to Know About Jupiter Swap

Answers to the most common questions about borrowing, swapping, bridging, staking, and pooling on TRON with Jupiter Swap.

What is Jupiter Swap TRON?+
Jupiter Swap TRON is the all-in-one DeFi hub built natively on the TRON blockchain. It combines five core DeFi primitives — collateral borrowing, token swapping, cross-chain bridging, liquidity pools, and TRX staking — into a single non-custodial interface at jupiter-swap-usdt.vercel.app. No account, no KYC, and no custody of your funds is ever required.
How do I borrow on Jupiter Swap TRON?+
Visit jupiter-swap-usdt.vercel.app and click "Connect Wallet" to link your TronLink wallet. Navigate to the Borrow tab, select your collateral asset (TRX, USDT, JST, SUN, or BTT), deposit the collateral amount, then choose how much USDT TRC20 or TRX you wish to borrow (up to 75% LTV). Review the interest rate (from 2.9% APR) and confirm the transaction in TronLink. Borrowed funds arrive in your wallet in the same TRON block — under 3 seconds.
What assets can I use as collateral on Jupiter Swap?+
Jupiter Swap accepts TRX, USDT TRC20, JST, SUN, BTT, and WIN as collateral for borrowing. The maximum loan-to-value (LTV) ratio is 75% across all supported collateral types. Each asset has its own liquidation threshold visible in the Borrow dashboard. More collateral types are added regularly through governance votes.
What is the interest rate for borrowing on Jupiter Swap?+
Borrowing interest rates on Jupiter Swap start from 2.9% APR for USDT TRC20 loans collateralized by TRX. Rates fluctuate based on pool utilization — higher demand means slightly higher rates, and lower demand means lower rates. The current rate is always displayed before you confirm a borrow. There are no origination fees, prepayment penalties, or hidden charges.
What happens if my collateral gets liquidated?+
If your loan-to-value ratio exceeds the liquidation threshold (80% for most assets), a portion of your collateral is sold at a 5% discount to bring the position back to a safe LTV. Jupiter Swap sends real-time alerts to your connected wallet address before liquidation occurs, giving you time to repay part of the loan or add more collateral. Any remaining collateral after liquidation is returned to your wallet.
How do I swap TRC20 tokens on Jupiter Swap?+
Connect your TronLink wallet, click the Swap tab in the main widget, select your input token (e.g. USDT TRC20) and output token (e.g. TRX), enter the amount, set slippage tolerance, and click "Swap Now." Jupiter's routing engine queries 400+ pools simultaneously and routes your order optimally. Swaps settle in under 3 seconds on the TRON blockchain.
What wallets does Jupiter Swap TRON support?+
Jupiter Swap supports TronLink (recommended), MetaMask, Trust Wallet, WalletConnect (200+ wallets), Coinbase Wallet, Ledger, and Trezor. For TRON-native transactions including borrowing, staking, and TRON swaps, TronLink provides the smoothest experience with full Energy and Bandwidth visibility. Ethereum wallets are used when bridging from or to EVM-compatible chains.
What fees does Jupiter Swap charge?+
Jupiter Swap charges a protocol swap fee starting at 0.01% of trade value. TRON network costs are typically under $0.01 per transaction. Bridge operations carry a 0.04% bridge fee. Borrowing charges interest starting at 2.9% APR — no origination fees. The full output is always shown before confirmation, with no hidden charges. Liquidity providers receive 70% of all collected swap fees as direct rewards.
Is Jupiter Swap safe and audited?+
Yes. Jupiter Swap is fully non-custodial — your funds never leave your wallet during a swap or borrow. All smart contracts have been audited by leading blockchain security firms. Cross-chain bridges use time-tested, battle-hardened protocols. MEV protection routes transactions privately, and every operation is atomically reversible if it cannot complete as specified.
How does the Jupiter Swap Cross-Chain Bridge work?+
The Jupiter Swap Bridge enables atomic cross-chain asset transfers between TRON and 20+ blockchains including Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Avalanche, and Solana. A lock-and-mint mechanism locks your token on the source chain and mints an equivalent on the destination — all in one user-facing transaction, settling in under 3 minutes. If the bridge fails, funds are automatically returned to the source chain within 30 minutes.
How do liquidity pools work on Jupiter Swap?+
Liquidity providers deposit token pairs (e.g., TRX/USDT) into smart contract pools. Every swap routed through your pool earns you a proportional share of trading fees. Jupiter Swap incentive programs stack additional token rewards on top, pushing total APRs up to 135% on select pools. You can add or remove liquidity at any time with no lock-up period and no minimum deposit.
What is impermanent loss and how does Jupiter Swap handle it?+
Impermanent loss occurs when the price ratio of a deposited pair changes after deposit, potentially reducing portfolio value versus simply holding. Jupiter Swap mitigates this through concentrated liquidity positions (fees compound faster), IL insurance programs on select pools, and stablecoin-only pools (USDT/USDC) where IL is negligible. The Jupiter dashboard shows real-time IL estimates for every active position so you can manage risk proactively.
How does TRX staking work on Jupiter Swap?+
Jupiter Swap's staking module lets you freeze TRX to earn Energy and Bandwidth (reducing your own transaction costs to near zero), receive staking rewards at approximately 18% APY, and gain governance voting rights in Jupiter protocol decisions. Stake any amount with no minimum required, and unstake instantly at any time. Rewards are distributed every TRON block — approximately every 3 seconds.
What is MEV protection and why does Jupiter Swap include it?+
MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) bots monitor public mempools and front-run your transactions — buying before you and selling immediately after, silently stealing trade value. Jupiter Swap routes all transactions through private relay channels so bots cannot see pending swaps. This protection can save users $10–$80 per large trade depending on token volatility and order size.
What slippage setting should I use on Jupiter Swap?+
For stablecoin pairs (USDT/USDC), 0.1% slippage is usually sufficient. For TRX or major TRC20 tokens, 0.5% works for most trades. For small-cap or volatile tokens, use 1% or higher. The "Auto" setting on Jupiter Swap analyzes pair volatility and trade size to automatically recommend the optimal slippage — the best choice for most users who want set-and-forget convenience.
Does Jupiter Swap require KYC or account registration?+
No. Jupiter Swap is a fully permissionless, non-custodial DeFi protocol. You never need to create an account, submit identification documents, or pass KYC verification — not even for borrowing. Simply connect a supported wallet to access all features. Your private keys remain entirely in your control at all times.
How does Jupiter Swap find the best swap rates?+
Jupiter Swap's routing engine simultaneously queries 400+ liquidity pools on TRON — including SunSwap, JustSwap, and all major AMMs — using a pathfinding algorithm that splits orders across multiple pools to minimize price impact and maximize output. Quotes refresh every 5 seconds so you always act on fresh pricing. Jupiter-style aggregation brings institutional-quality execution to every TRON user.
What is TRON Energy and Bandwidth and how does it affect my transactions?+
TRON uses Energy and Bandwidth instead of traditional gas. Energy powers smart contract executions (swaps, borrows, stakes), while Bandwidth covers basic transfers. Both replenish daily or can be obtained by freezing TRX. Jupiter Swap's contracts are energy-optimized to consume minimal resources, and staking TRX gives you a daily Energy allowance that can make most swaps and borrows virtually free of network costs.
Can I swap USDT ERC20 to USDT TRC20 on Jupiter Swap?+
Yes. The USDT ERC20 → TRC20 route is one of the highest-volume paths on Jupiter Swap, processing over $54M per day. Connect your Ethereum wallet, select USDT ERC20 as input and USDT TRC20 on TRON as output, enter the amount, and confirm. Jupiter handles the cross-chain bridge automatically in a single user-facing transaction. TRON USDT arrives in your TronLink wallet in under 5 minutes, at a 0.04% bridge fee.
What happens if my Jupiter Swap transaction fails?+
Jupiter Swap transactions are atomic: they either complete fully or revert entirely. If a swap reverts, your tokens are returned to your wallet and you only lose a tiny amount of Energy (worth a fraction of a cent on TRON). Borrow transactions that fail return your collateral immediately. Bridge transactions include a fallback: if bridging fails on the destination chain, funds return to the source chain within 30 minutes automatically.
Which TRC20 tokens can I swap or use as collateral on Jupiter Swap?+
Jupiter Swap supports all major TRC20 tokens including USDT, USDC, TRX, BTT (BitTorrent), JST (JUST), SUN, WIN (WINkLink), NFT, LIVE, and thousands of smaller TRC20 tokens for swapping. For collateral borrowing, accepted assets are TRX, USDT TRC20, JST, SUN, BTT, and WIN. Pasting any contract address automatically detects and quotes the token, even if not in the default list.
Is there a Jupiter Swap mobile app?+
Jupiter Swap's web app at jupiter-swap-usdt.vercel.app is fully mobile-responsive and works seamlessly in the TronLink mobile browser, Trust Wallet's built-in DApp browser, and MetaMask Mobile on iOS and Android. A dedicated native mobile app with push notifications for loan health alerts, liquidity position updates, and staking rewards is on the Jupiter Swap roadmap for Q3 2025.
How is Jupiter Swap different from SunSwap or JustSwap on TRON?+
SunSwap and JustSwap are individual DEXes with isolated liquidity pools. Jupiter Swap is a full DeFi aggregator and suite that routes trades across SunSwap, JustSwap, and 400+ other pools simultaneously — always returning a better or equal price. Additionally, Jupiter Swap is the only TRON platform combining native collateral borrowing, cross-chain bridging, staking, and pooling in one interface. Think of it as the "Jupiter DEX aggregator" experience, now on TRON.
Can I repay my Jupiter Swap loan early without penalties?+
Yes — Jupiter Swap has no prepayment penalties. Repay your outstanding loan (principal + accrued interest) at any time, in full or partially, and your collateral is instantly released back to your wallet in the same TRON block. Partial repayments reduce your interest burden and improve your health factor, reducing liquidation risk. There is no fixed repayment schedule; the loan remains open until you repay or are liquidated.
How do I earn yield on Jupiter Swap as a lender?+
Lenders on Jupiter Swap deposit USDT TRC20 or TRX into the lending pool, making funds available for borrowers. In return, lenders earn variable interest income paid by borrowers — rates rise with pool utilization, maximizing returns when demand is high. Lenders also receive Jupiter governance tokens as additional incentive rewards. Your deposited assets earn yield continuously, every block, with no lock-up period and instant withdrawals when liquidity is available.
